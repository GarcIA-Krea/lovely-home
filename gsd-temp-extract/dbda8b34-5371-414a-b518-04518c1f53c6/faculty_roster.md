# GSD AI Academy: The Faculty & The Fleet

> **Concept**: The **Faculty** are the *Master Agents* who run the Academy. The **Maximi** are the *Personal Agents* that students build and take with them to work.

---

## 1. The Faculty (The Masters)
*These agents live in the "Campus". They teach, grade, and govern.*

### **The Architect (The Connector)**
*   **Visual**: A Scholar in flowing robes, holding a giant Calligraphy Brush.
*   **Role**: The Engineer.
*   **Skill**: **MCP orchestration**. connects NotebookLM to the LMS.
*   **Job**: "You need a course on Denial Management? I will ink the scroll and build the module."

### **The Hunter (The Scout)**
*   **Visual**: A Silent Archer or Falcon. Accurate, swift.
*   **Role**: The Researcher.
*   **Skill**: **Deep Web Search / EMR Parsing**.
*   **Job**: "I found a new update to the Epic system manual. I am updating the Knowledge Base now."

### **The Compliance Officer (The Shield)**
*   **Visual**: A Stone Guardian (Foo Dog). Immovable, heavy.
*   **Role**: The Auditor.
*   **Skill**: **Regex / Pattern Matching**.
*   **Job**: "Stop. You are about to paste a patient name into a public chat. I have blocked this action."

---

## 2. The Fleet (The Product: "Maximi")
*These are the personalized agents every Orchestrator gets after graduation.*

### **Maximi-RCM (The Revenue Bot)**
*   **Visual**: A Golden Abacus or Koi Fish (wealth).
*   **User**: Billing Specialist.
*   **Specialty**: CPT Codes, Denial Appeals.

### **Maximi-Triage (The Intake Bot)**
*   **Visual**: A Lotus Flower (calm/unfolding).
*   **User**: Patient Scheduler.
*   **Specialty**: Symptom checking, Provider matching.

### **Maximi-Scribe (The Documentation Bot)**
*   **Visual**: A Floating Scroll.
*   **User**: Virtual Scribe.
*   **Specialty**: EMR Navigation, Note formatting.
